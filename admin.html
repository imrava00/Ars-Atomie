Optional: Google Drive folder link

If all of your documents already sit in one Google Drive folder, paste the share link of that folder here and students get a single Open folder button at the top of the lecture page. This is only a shortcut. Adding materials one by one in the sections below gives students a much nicer organized page, so most of the time you can skip this.

1Section details how the section appears on the lecture page
2Materials paste a Google Drive link for each document

How to get the link: in Google Drive, right click the document, choose Share, set General access to Anyone with the link (Viewer), then copy the link and paste it here. Documents shared this way open right inside the lecture page.
